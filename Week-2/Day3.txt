Notes for Bandit Level 15 - 16

Objective was submit the password on the localhost on port 30001 using TLS encryption.

What confused me/ What are my mistakes:

	Syntax mistakes:
		openssl s_client -crlf \ connect gibson-0:30001
	Confusion:
		I didnt put a flag into the connect and servername 
		Flags = "-"

The solution:

	openssl s_client -crlf -connect gibson-0:30001 -servername gibson-0
	Then feed the password into it (The level 15 password the one used when logging in)
