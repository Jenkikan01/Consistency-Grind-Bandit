Notes in Bandit Consistency Levels

Notes for Bandit Level 6 to Level 7:

What confused me:
	I got confused on the part where flags are involved.
	I forgot what flags belongs to group and user on the find command.

What I did:
	I check out the man pages and found out the following:
	the flags for find command are the following:
	    -group = owned by groups
	    -user = owned by user
	    -size = for the size of the file

Mistakes:
	I used only the following syntax:
	    find / -user bandit7 -group bandit6 -size 33c
	The mistake in here is errors will be included in the output 

Solution:
	2>/dev/null shall be included in the end... so it would be like this:
	find / -user bandit7 -group bandit6 -size 33c 2>/dev/null

	2>/dev/null is used to filter out the errors and find what we really are looking for
	
	Then read the contents/password by using the following:
	cat /var/lib/dpkg/info/bandit7.password
